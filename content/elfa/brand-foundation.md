## What ELFA is

ELFA is a developing fashion project within the BY ALAMIR archive. Its work centers on garments, materials, proportion, construction, and visual identity, with an emphasis on making familiar clothing feel more considered through detail rather than excess.

## How it began

ELFA grew out of repeated garment and identity experiments rather than a single finished collection. Individual ideas around silhouette, textile choice, closures, collars, surface language, and branding gradually began to share the same design logic. What started as separate clothing concepts became a broader fashion project with its own name and point of view.

The project remains intentionally in development. The portfolio documents the foundation first; collections, garments, and other public-facing extensions can be added as they become defined enough to represent the project accurately.

## Mission

**ELEVATED LUXURY FOR ALL.**

The name describes the central design position behind ELFA: luxury should be communicated through care, material, proportion, construction, and experience rather than through exclusivity alone. “For All” is not a claim that every future object must occupy the same price point. It is a refusal to make exclusion the thing that gives an object value.

ELFA aims to create clothing that feels elevated because it has been considered closely — clothing meant to be worn, lived in, and understood through its details.

## Working rule

Elevate the object. Keep the person at the center.
